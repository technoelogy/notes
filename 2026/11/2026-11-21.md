# 2026-11-21 — Next Rupee Planner at Claude Code Impact Lab Mumbai

## Shipped
Next Rupee Planner at the Claude Code Impact Lab hackathon in Mumbai, with Prathik Shetty, on a synthetic BMC road-repair dataset. It ranks every open road issue in a ward by risk and shows what the remaining budget should fix first, with the arithmetic behind each score. Ward F/N (Matunga, Sion, Wadala): ₹10.9 lakh left, 39 open issues, ₹14 lakh to fix them all, so 25 get funded and 14 wait. My side: UI, the audit, contractor and complaint-trail pages that back the planner up, and the Guide tour and cross-links between them. Live at rupeespeaks.vercel.app. Also recorded a 93 s ward F/N walkthrough (AI voiceover, driven through the Guide). LinkedIn post and thumbnail are ready, not posted yet.
